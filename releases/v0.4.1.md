# Papo Mobile 0.4.1

Papo Mobile 0.4.1 brings the server's animated emoji to life and shows who
reacted to a message. Emoji in GIF and WebP move in messages, reactions and the
emoji picker, and the server's emoji show as images in the message box instead
of their `:name:`.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Emoji

- Animated emoji from the server (GIF and WebP) now move in messages,
  reactions and the emoji picker, larger when a message has only emoji. They
  stop while the app is in the background, and stay on their first frame in the
  suggestions after `:` and when Android has animations removed.
- The server's emoji now show as images in the message box, moving like they do
  in messages. Each one counts as one character when you move the cursor, delete
  or select, and the message still goes out as `:name:`, so other clients see
  the same emoji.

## Reactions

- Hold a reaction, or choose "Ver reações" in a message's actions, to see who
  reacted, with a list for each emoji. You can see it in any channel you can
  read, even where you cannot react. The list is read when it opens.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, profile and status editing, user preferences, blocking
  users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- Video attachments play only after being downloaded, and attached GIFs, link
  preview images and APNG emoji show only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
