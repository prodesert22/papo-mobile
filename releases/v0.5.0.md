# Papo Mobile 0.5.0

Papo Mobile 0.5.0 plays videos inside the app. Attached videos and videos from
links open full screen and start while they download, with seeking, and their
cards show a cover. When a video cannot play, the app says why and offers the
next step.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Videos

- Video attachments play inside the app while they download. Tap a video's card
  to watch it full screen, seek anywhere in it and turn the phone without losing
  your place; the video pauses when you leave the app.
- Videos from links play the same way: a link preview with a playable video
  shows a play button, and nothing is downloaded until you tap it.
- Video cards show a cover: the server's thumbnail when there is one, otherwise
  a frame the phone takes from the start of the video, reading only a small part
  of it. A ring around the play button shows while the cover is being made, and
  covers are kept encrypted on the phone so they appear at once next time.
- When a video cannot play, the viewer says why: the session ended, you have no
  access, the format is not supported on this phone, or the server could not
  deliver it. It offers to try again where that may help, **Baixar** for
  attachments, and opening the page in the browser for a link whose video is
  gone.
- Nothing from a video is written to the phone except its cover, which is
  encrypted like the rest of the app's data.

## Fixes

- An image, avatar or video cover could stay blank for the rest of the session
  after you scrolled past it while it was still loading; it now loads again when
  it comes back into view.

## Permissions

- The app now declares Android's `WAKE_LOCK` permission, which the video player
  uses to keep the phone awake while a video plays. Android grants it at
  install, without asking.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, profile and status editing, user preferences, blocking
  users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- Videos do not keep playing in the background, and attached GIFs, link preview
  images and APNG emoji show only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
