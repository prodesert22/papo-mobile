# Papo Mobile 0.4.0

Papo Mobile 0.4.0 follows the server's permissions. Channels you cannot read no
longer show, a channel where you can only read says so in place of the message
box, and the app offers only the actions you may take instead of letting the
server refuse them with an error. `@everyone` arrives for those allowed to use
it, highlighted in gold as in the web client.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Permissions

- Channels you cannot read no longer show in the channel list, the quick
  switcher or the search filters, and their messages are removed from the
  phone. A draft you had there is kept and comes back if you regain access.
- A channel where you can only read shows that in place of the message box. You
  can still edit and delete your own messages and take back your own
  reactions there.
- Actions you may not take are no longer offered instead of failing with an
  error: attaching files, pinning, reacting, replying and joining a call each
  follow your permissions. In direct messages you can always pin.
- Moderators and the owner can delete other people's messages from a message's
  actions, behind the same confirmation as your own.
- If you lose access to a channel while it is open, the app says so instead of
  showing its history, and loads it again when access comes back.
- Changes to your roles apply as soon as the server announces them, and the
  rest when the app reconnects.

## @everyone

- In a channel where you may notify everyone, `@everyone` comes first when you
  type `@`. It is not offered in direct messages, where everyone is notified
  anyway.
- In messages, `@everyone` shows highlighted and the message turns gold only
  when its author may notify everyone, as in the web client; this includes your
  own messages.

## Fixes

- Taking back your own reaction could leave the old count on the message until
  it was opened again.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, profile and status editing, user preferences, blocking
  users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- Video attachments play only after being downloaded, and GIFs and animated
  emoji show only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
