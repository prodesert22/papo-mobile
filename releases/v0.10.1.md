# Papo Mobile 0.10.1

Papo Mobile 0.10.1 shows in the channel list where you are: the conversation
you have open and the voice channel of your call.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## The channel list

- The channel list shows where you are. The conversation open in the panel,
  a channel or a direct conversation, has a gray background and its name in a
  stronger color, also while you look at the list with the conversation
  swiped aside. The voice channel of your call has the green of the call bar
  and a phone icon in place of the speaker, from joining until you leave.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
