# Papo Mobile 0.7.0

Papo Mobile 0.7.0 is about your own account. You can edit your profile with an
image editor that lets you choose the part of the picture to keep, choose your
status, block people, change your password or reset it with a link, and see the
details of your server. The top of the channel list was reworked around your
avatar and status, and the back gesture now waits for you to confirm.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Your profile

- Edit your own profile from the row under the server name at the top of the
  channel list, from your profile or from the account settings: nickname, status
  message, description and typing phrase, plus your avatar and banner from the
  gallery.
- The editor is laid out like a profile card: the banner and the avatar show in
  place, each with a pencil button to change it, and the Save button sits next to
  the title, where it stays in view. A floating notice warns about unsaved
  changes and offers to discard them. The result of each change is shown, and a
  change the server may or may not have applied is never sent again on its own.
- Picking an avatar or banner opens an image editor: move and zoom the picture
  under a fixed frame (square for the avatar, 3:1 for the banner), rotate it, or
  reset it with "Tamanho". Pictures are shrunk on the phone and lose the
  location they carry. You can remove your banner. Animated pictures are not
  cropped: they go as they are when they fit the size limits.
- Choose Available, Away or Busy from the profile editor or from your own
  profile, with a disc in the color of each status.

## Blocking and password

- Block people from their profile and manage the list in the account settings.
  Blocking removes the direct conversation and stops direct messages in both
  directions; channel messages stay visible. The new conversation list hides the
  people you blocked and says so when the other person blocked you.
- Change your password from the account settings. Every session of the account
  ends, this one included, and the app takes you to sign in again.
- Reset your password with a link from the sign-in screen, with a warning when
  the link's address differs from the server's.

## The account and the channel list

- The account settings show the server's details: name, icon, public or closed,
  owner, and how many members, channels and roles it has.
- The top of the channel list was reworked: the server name has room and wraps to
  a second line instead of being cut off, the account settings button is a
  vertical three-dot menu, and a new row shows your avatar, a disc with the color
  of your status (green available, amber away, red busy, gray offline) and your
  name. Tapping that row opens the profile editor.

## Navigation

- The back gesture no longer moves the screen while your finger is still down:
  the conversation and the other screens only slide out after you confirm going
  back. The Android preview of the screen behind, and the animation back to the
  home screen, are gone with it.

## Fixes

- The account nickname no longer loses its last character when you leave the
  settings right after typing, and picking a color no longer reverts it.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, user preferences and server administration.
- Removing your avatar once set (only replacing it), and a status of "invisible".
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- A camera in the attachment menu, and removing a photo's location.
- Videos and audio do not keep playing in the background, and APNG emoji show
  only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
