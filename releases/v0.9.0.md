# Papo Mobile 0.9.0

Papo Mobile 0.9.0 reorganizes the app around you. Settings become lists like
Android's own, with themes (OLED included) for the whole app or for each
server, new privacy choices and an about screen with the licenses. The channel
list splits channels and direct conversations, channels show their full
description and can be edited by those allowed, and you can record voice
messages. The phone also keeps less history, fetching older messages from the
server when you need them.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Settings

- Settings are lists with a short summary of what is inside each item, as in
  Android's own settings. The gear at the bottom of the server bar opens the
  app settings, and the menu at the top of the channel list opens the settings
  of that server's account.
- "Notificações do Android" in the app settings opens the system notification
  settings, where the sound and vibration of each channel live.

## Appearance

- A theme for the whole app: follow the system, light, dark or OLED, with a
  black background and black list areas to save energy on OLED screens. Six
  presets (Papo, the current look, plus Oceano, Floresta, Ametista, Âmbar and
  Rosa) and a live preview of the channel list and a conversation before you
  choose.
- Change each color of a theme: accent, background, list areas, server bar,
  text, secondary text, mentions and the mention highlight, with a color
  wheel, a `#RRGGBB` code or quick swatches, and the preview following every
  change. A warning shows when the text gets hard to read, and each color can
  go back to the preset's.
- A server can have its own theme, used while that account is selected; the
  lock screen and adding a server always use the app's theme.

## Security and privacy

- Screenshots and screen recording can be allowed or blocked with or without
  the app lock. Until you choose, nothing changes: they are blocked with the
  lock on and allowed without it.
- The app can lock 15 minutes or an hour after you leave it, besides right away
  and after 1 or 5 minutes.
- Choose what the notifications the app shows reveal: everything, nothing on
  the phone's lock screen, or only "Nova mensagem" everywhere, without the
  author or the picture. Push notifications shown by Android follow the
  server's preview instead.

## Channels and direct conversations

- The channel list has two tabs, "Canais" and "Conversas diretas", each with its
  mention count. Without direct conversations, a button in the middle of the
  list starts one.
- Close a direct conversation by holding it in the list. It leaves the list but
  keeps its history: a new message, or starting it again, brings it back.
- In a direct conversation, typing `@` suggests only its two people, you and
  the other person, instead of everyone on the server.
- Tap the description under a channel's name to read it in full.
- People allowed to manage channels can rename a channel and change its
  description, from the conversation menu, from the description or by holding
  the channel in the list. Nobody else sees the option.
- Holding a channel shows its name at the top of the sheet, with the channel
  settings, when you may change them, above the notification settings.
- The server bar shows your avatar on each account, instead of the first letter
  of your name.
- A server without a connection for more than two seconds shows a
  disconnected icon at the top right of its picture in the server bar and next
  to its name above the channel list.

## Voice messages

- Record a voice message from the clip menu in a conversation: Papo asks for
  the microphone, records up to 30 minutes, and after you stop you can listen
  to it, discard it or send it. It is sent as its own audio message, and it
  plays here and on the web.

## Storage on the phone

- The phone now keeps only the 120 newest messages of the 3 conversations you
  opened last on each account, with their images, GIFs and video posters.
  Older history and every other conversation are removed from the phone and
  fetched from the server when you open them. Videos were never stored and
  still are not. The first time you open the app after updating, the stored
  images of messages and other cached media are cleaned up; avatars and custom
  emojis stay.

## About the app

- "Sobre o Papo Mobile" in the app settings: the version, beta and build
  number with a button to copy them, links to the source code, the releases
  and the server, web and terminal projects, and the app's license, the GNU
  AGPL v3.0, to read inside the app.
- "Licenças de terceiros" lists the libraries the app ships with their
  licenses, and the font and emoji data it includes, without going online.

## Fixes

- An avatar with a transparent background no longer shows a colored circle
  behind it in conversations, profiles and the member list. The account tile in
  the server bar keeps the account color behind it.
- The keyboard no longer pops up for a moment after you pick an item from the
  conversation menu. It now closes when you open the menu, the member list, a
  profile, a message's actions, reactions, an image or a video full screen, the
  quick switcher or a call from the conversation.

## Not in this release

- Server administration, beyond renaming a channel and changing its
  description.
- Closing a direct conversation reaches your other devices only when they load
  the list again, and a server's own theme stays on this phone.
- A voice message cannot be recorded during a voice call, and leaving the app
  stops the recording; it is discarded when you leave the conversation or the
  app locks.
- Notifications that follow your mute and hiding choices with the app closed:
  those arrive only while the app is open. With the app closed only the
  server's push arrives, and only on servers that have it turned on.
- Mute, the server-wide level and hiding `@everyone` or mentions on your other
  devices: the server does not store them, so they stay on this phone.
- Removing your avatar once set (only replacing it), and a status of "invisible".
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- A camera in the attachment menu, and removing a photo's location.
- Videos and audio do not keep playing in the background, and APNG emoji show
  only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
