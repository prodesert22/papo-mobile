# Papo Mobile 0.1.0

Papo Mobile 0.1.0 is the first release of the Android client for Papo. It signs
in to several servers and accounts at once, keeps everything it stores on the
phone encrypted behind an optional fingerprint lock, and covers text chat, voice
calls, camera and screen sharing.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Servers and accounts

- Add as many servers as you want, each by its own address, and switch between
  them in the rail on the left. Every account keeps its own session, history
  and media, and nothing is shared between them.
- Sign in to the same server with more than one account. Each one shows the
  server icon with a badge carrying the account's initial; give an account a
  nickname and a color in **Configurações da conta** to tell them apart.
- Closed servers ask for the server password before you sign in or create an
  account.
- An `http://` address shows a warning that passwords and messages travel
  unencrypted, and connects only after you confirm it.
- When a server ends your session, the account is marked **Sessão encerrada**
  and **Entrar de novo** takes you straight back to its sign-in.
- **Dispositivos conectados** lists the sessions of an account and signs out
  one of them or all at once.

## Privacy on the device

- **Bloquear com digital** locks the app behind your fingerprint or the phone's
  screen lock. The key that opens the stored data is kept in the phone's secure
  hardware and is only released after you authenticate, so the data cannot be
  read without it.
- Choose when the app locks after you leave it: at once, after 1 minute or after
  5 minutes. While the lock is on, **Esconder conteúdo fora do app** blocks
  screenshots and hides conversations in the recent apps list.
- Accounts, sessions and messages are stored in encrypted databases, and cached
  images and avatars are encrypted too. None of it is included in device
  backups.
- Signing out of an account removes its messages and media from the phone.

## Messages

- Text channels grouped by category and direct messages, updated in real time.
  Unread channels and conversations are marked, and so is every account in the
  rail that has something unread.
- Scroll back through the history; it loads as you go and keeps what you have
  already read available offline.
- Send text with attachments, reply to a message, and edit or delete your own.
- Markdown formatting: bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code, code blocks,
  headings, quotes and links.
- Mention people with `@`; a list of matching names appears as you type.
- React with emoji, including the server's own, and pin messages. **Mensagens
  fixadas** lists what is pinned in a channel.
- Images show a thumbnail and open full screen with pinch to zoom. Other files
  download to the phone's Downloads folder. Images the server marks as
  sensitive stay hidden until you tap them.
- Link previews appear below the message with their title, description and
  image.
- See who is typing and who is online, away or busy.

## Voice, camera and screen sharing

- Tap a voice channel to join the call. You join with the microphone on;
  mute, turn the camera on, switch between the front and back cameras, and
  choose between the speaker and the earpiece from the call screen.
- Share your screen with the call.
- See the camera and the shared screen of other participants. Tap a video to
  enlarge it, and tap again to return to the grid. Whoever is speaking is
  outlined.
- A call continues when you leave the app. A notification stays visible while
  it is active and can hang up, and a bar above the channel list takes you back
  to it.
- One call at a time: joining another voice channel leaves the current one.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- Message search, the notification inbox, profile and status editing, user
  preferences, blocking users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset
  selection in calls.
- Video attachments play only after being downloaded, GIFs are not animated,
  and the server's custom emoji are not rendered inside message text.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
