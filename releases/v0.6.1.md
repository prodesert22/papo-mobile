# Papo Mobile 0.6.1

Papo Mobile 0.6.1 changes how you move between a conversation and the channel
list. The conversation is now a panel over the list, as in Discord: it follows
your finger both ways, and the app opens straight into a conversation instead of
the list.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Moving between a conversation and the channel list

- The conversation is now a panel over the channel list, as in Discord. Swipe it
  to the right to see the channels, and the conversation hides completely;
  swipe the channel list to the left to bring it back. The panel follows your
  finger, can be caught in the middle of the movement, and settles open or
  closed when you let go. Opening a channel, the back button and the back
  gesture use the same panel.
- Swiping a message to the left still replies to it.
- Each account remembers its own last conversation: switching accounts in the
  side rail switches the conversation waiting off screen, and an account with
  none shows only its channel list.
- Messages that arrive in a conversation waiting off screen stay unread in the
  list until you open it again.
- A conversation opened from a search result or from the call screen now opens
  in the panel, so going back shows the channel list instead of the search
  results or the call.

## Opening the app

- Opening the app, unlocking it or coming back to it with the channel list on
  screen now opens a conversation of the selected account: the one you had open
  there, or, if none, the first text channel at the top of the channel list, as
  soon as the channels arrive from the server.
- Coming back to a conversation or to another screen leaves you where you were.
  An account with no text channel you can read, or whose session has ended,
  stays on the channel list, and so does the app while it has no connection.

## Messages

- A little space now separates the newest message from the message box.

## Not in this release

- Opening the app in the last conversation you used after the app was closed
  completely: for now a fresh start opens the top channel.
- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, profile and status editing, user preferences, blocking
  users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- A camera in the attachment menu, and removing a photo's location.
- Videos and audio do not keep playing in the background, and APNG emoji show
  only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
