# Papo Mobile 0.8.0

Papo Mobile 0.8.0 is about notifications. Each account gets a notification
inbox and mention counts, you choose what notifies you for each server and
channel, and mentions, replies and direct messages now show in the Android
notification shade. On servers with push turned on, they also arrive with the
app closed.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## The inbox and mention counts

- A notification inbox for each account, opened from the bell at the top of the
  channel list: mentions, replies to your messages, `@everyone` and direct
  messages, with the reason for each one. Opening a notification takes you to
  the message and marks it as read, and you can mark all the loaded ones as
  read.
- Mention counts: a red number next to each channel and direct conversation, on
  each account in the server bar and on the bell. A channel with new messages
  shows its name in white and a dot on the right.

## Notification settings

- Notification settings for each server and for each channel (hold the channel
  in the list or use the menu in the conversation): which messages notify (all,
  mentions only or nothing), mute for 15 minutes up to 24 hours or until you
  turn it back on, and hide `@everyone` or direct mentions. Channels use the
  server's choices until you change them. Direct conversations can be muted the
  same way.
- A muted channel is dimmed and loses its new-message dot, but its mention count
  stays. Hiding `@everyone` or mentions removes them from the count too.
- A channel's notification level is saved on the server, so the terminal and
  web clients follow it, unless you turn off "Sincronizar com o servidor" for
  that channel or for the whole server: then the phone keeps its own level and
  the other clients keep theirs. Mute and hiding `@everyone` or mentions stay on
  this phone.

## System notifications

- System notifications while the app is open: mentions, replies, `@everyone`
  and direct messages show in the Android notification shade, one per
  conversation with the latest messages and each author's picture, grouped by
  account and following the notification settings. Tapping one opens the
  message, after the unlock if the app lock is on.
- Each account has its own Android notification channels (mentions and replies,
  direct messages, messages), where you can change the sound and vibration. The
  app asks for the notification permission once, on Android 13 or later.
- Push notifications with the app closed or in the background, when the server
  has push turned on. Android shows them by itself: the level saved on the
  server decides which messages arrive and the text follows the server's
  preview setting, but mute and hiding `@everyone` or mentions do not apply to
  them. Tapping one opens the conversation, after the unlock if the app lock is
  on. Two accounts on the same server cannot both receive pushes on one phone.

## The conversation

- The conversation's search and pinned messages moved into the three-dot menu
  at the top, next to the new notification settings, so the conversation name
  has room again on narrow screens.

## Fixes

- A channel or direct conversation no longer looks unread after its newest
  message is deleted.

## Not in this release

- Notifications that follow your mute and hiding choices with the app closed:
  those arrive only while the app is open. With the app closed only the
  server's push arrives, and only on servers that have it turned on.
- Mute, the server-wide level and hiding `@everyone` or mentions on your other
  devices: the server does not store them, so they stay on this phone.
- Reading on another device reaches the phone only when the app reconnects or
  comes back to the front, and a push notification already in the shade stays
  until you open or dismiss it. Push notifications have no author picture.
- Server administration.
- Removing your avatar once set (only replacing it), and a status of "invisible".
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- A camera in the attachment menu, and removing a photo's location.
- Videos and audio do not keep playing in the background, and APNG emoji show
  only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
