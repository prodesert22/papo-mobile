# Papo Mobile 0.9.4

Papo Mobile 0.9.4 shows mentions as you type them and fixes mentions of
usernames with a space.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Messages

- While you type, a mention in the message field shows in the mention color on
  a light background, so you can see that the person will be notified;
  `@everyone` is highlighted too when you may use it.

## Fixes

- Mentioning someone whose username has a space, such as `@yog Sothoth`, now
  reaches them: the message is sent as a mention and they get the notification,
  instead of the name going out as plain text.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
