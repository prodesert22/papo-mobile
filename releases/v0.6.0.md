# Papo Mobile 0.6.0

Papo Mobile 0.6.0 is about media and people. Photos, videos and GIFs go out
straight from the gallery or the keyboard, link previews show everything the
server sends, and a message's images and videos open in a viewer you can swipe
through. Videos play in the message itself, with new controls in full screen,
YouTube plays inside the app, and audio attachments get a player. You can also
open a person's profile and browse the server's members.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Attachments

- Attach photos and videos straight from the gallery: the paperclip offers
  **Foto ou vídeo** (the system photo picker, no storage permission) and
  **Arquivo**. Photos and videos show a preview before sending, GIFs carry a
  GIF badge, and tapping a photo's preview shows it whole.
- GIFs, stickers and images from the keyboard, or pasted, come into the message
  as attachments, like files picked with the paperclip.
- A message takes up to 10 files, 100 MB each and 105 MB together; what does not
  fit is refused with a notice before sending, and what you typed and attached
  stays.
- A JPEG photo that carries its GPS location shows "Esta foto tem a sua
  localização" before you send it.
- Attachments are uploaded as a stream instead of being read into memory, so
  large videos send with a progress bar and can be cancelled. A file that
  disappears midway, or a message the server refuses for its size, fails with a
  clear reason.
- Attached GIFs move in the message list and in the viewer, with a GIF badge.
- Attachments removed by the server's moderation show "Removido pela
  moderação" instead of failing, and an attachment that becomes sensitive after
  you opened it is hidden again.

## Link previews

- Link previews show everything the server sends: a color bar, the author with
  an icon and a link, a linked title, the description with formatting (cut at
  six lines, with **Mostrar mais**), fields in a grid, and the footer.
- GIF links (Tenor, GIPHY, KLIPY) play in the message as an animated GIF with
  the provider and title, and preview images that are GIFs move too. Tapping the
  GIF opens it in the viewer; the provider and title open its page.
- Messages sent by a webhook carry an **APP** badge next to the name and show
  the webhook's avatar, in conversations, pinned messages and search results. An
  author picture that is an address on another site is not loaded; those
  messages keep the initials.

## Viewer

- A message's images, GIFs and videos open in a viewer you can swipe through,
  with a counter, pinch and double-tap zoom up to 5x, and **Salvar** and
  **Compartilhar** for images and GIFs. Link preview images open there too,
  after the attachments, so a message with several previews can be swiped
  through.
- Sharing hands the file to the other app from memory, without writing it to
  the phone.

## Videos and audio

- Videos, attached or from a link, play in the message itself when you tap the
  play button in the middle, clean, without controls; tapping the playing video
  opens it full screen from the same point, and tapping anywhere else on a video
  that is not playing opens it full screen too. Only one video plays at a time
  in a conversation, and a video that scrolls off the screen stops.
- The full-screen video player has new controls in the app's style: a large
  play and pause button, back and forward 10 seconds, a gold progress bar that
  shows how much has loaded and seeks when you let go, and controls and title
  that hide on their own while the video plays and come back with a tap.
- YouTube links show the video's image with a play button. Tapping it plays the
  video inside the app, in the message; tapping elsewhere on the card opens it
  full screen, and **Abrir no YouTube** opens it outside the app. Nothing is
  requested from YouTube until you tap play.
- Audio attachments play in the message, with play and pause, a seek bar, the
  elapsed time, 1x, 1.5x and 2x speed and **Baixar**. Voice recordings made on
  the web play too. A bar at the top of the conversation list and of the
  conversation keeps the playing audio at hand when its message is out of view;
  audio pauses when you leave the app and stops when you switch accounts or the
  app locks.

## Links

- Addresses that start with `www.` are now links.
- Hold a message to see where each of its links really goes, with buttons to
  copy or open it, and a link whose text hides its address (`[text](address)`)
  shows the address and asks before opening.

## Profiles and members

- Tap a person's avatar or name in a conversation, or the title of a direct
  conversation, to open their profile: banner, avatar, presence, status, roles,
  description and the date they joined. From there you can open a direct
  conversation with them or copy their `@username`.
- A member list for the server: the new button at the top of a channel opens a
  panel from the right edge with everyone grouped by presence and a search by
  name. Tap a person to open their profile. Swipe it back, tap outside it or
  press back to close it.

## Navigation

- Screens now slide in from the right over the previous one and slide back out
  the same way. With the back gesture the screen follows your finger instead of
  shrinking toward the middle, and comes back if you let go early.

## Fixes

- Link preview images and video covers were cut off at the sides: they now show
  whole, in their own proportion, and tall ones are narrower instead of cropped.
- The avatar of a person who has none was requested from the server again every
  time it came into view.

## Privacy

- The YouTube player is the one place where the app stores something
  unencrypted: while it is open, the phone's web component keeps its cookies and
  cache in the app's folder. They are deleted when the player closes and when
  the app locks; a few empty databases named after YouTube stay until the app is
  next opened, when they are removed.
- The YouTube player needs Android System WebView 133 or newer; with an older
  one, YouTube videos open outside the app.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, profile and status editing, user preferences, blocking
  users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- A camera in the attachment menu, and removing a photo's location.
- Videos and audio do not keep playing in the background, and APNG emoji show
  only their first frame.
- Messages waiting to be sent, and files attached to a draft, are lost when the
  app locks or closes.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
