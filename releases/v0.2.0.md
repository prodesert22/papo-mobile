# Papo Mobile 0.2.0

Papo Mobile 0.2.0 adds message search and a full emoji panel. Search finds
messages across the server or inside one conversation and opens any result in
place, however old it is. The emoji panel takes the keyboard's place, lists every
Unicode emoji together with the server's own, and the server's emoji now show as
images inside messages.

It needs Android 11 or newer and a Papo server to connect to. The interface is
in Portuguese.

## Message search

- Search messages on the server. Tap the magnifier in the account header to
  search the whole server, or the one in a conversation to search only there.
  Type what you are looking for and tap **Buscar**; filter by conversation,
  author, mentioned person, links, attachments and a range of dates, and choose
  between newest and oldest first. Results come 100 at a time, with **Carregar
  mais** for the next page.
- The server-wide search does not reach direct messages. To search one, pick it
  in **Conversa** or start the search from inside it.
- Tap a search result to open its conversation at that message, highlighted,
  with the history around it, however old it is. While you read an old stretch
  of a conversation, scrolling loads more in both directions and **Ir para as
  recentes** takes you back to the latest messages.
- **Filtros recentes** offers the filters of your last five searches again. They
  are kept only in memory and are gone when the app locks or closes.
- **Mensagens fixadas** has a field that filters the list by text or author as
  you type.
- Searching for a person or a conversation by name ignores accents: `joao` finds
  "João".

## Emoji

- The smiley button beside **Enviar** opens an emoji panel in place of the
  keyboard and at the keyboard's height; the keyboard button in the same spot
  brings the keyboard back without the message box moving. Drag the panel up to
  open it over the whole conversation, or down to close it; **Back** also closes
  it.
- The panel lists every Unicode emoji by category together with the server's own
  emoji, and searches by name without caring about accents or case, in
  Portuguese and English (`joia`, `coracao`, `thumbsup`), with the results kept
  above the keyboard while you type. Picking an emoji inserts it at the cursor
  and leaves the panel open for more.
- **+** on a message's reaction row opens the same emoji list in a sheet that
  starts at half the screen and opens fully when dragged up or when you search.
- The emoji you used last come first, in the panel and in the quick reactions,
  and are kept encrypted on the phone for each account.
- Type `:` and the start of a name in the message box to get emoji suggestions,
  with the server's own emoji first. Typing the closing colon of a complete name,
  such as `:+1:`, turns it into the emoji; a name that is also one of the
  server's emoji stays as text so it shows as that emoji's image.
- The server's custom emoji written as `:name:` show as images inside messages,
  including in bold and quotes but not in code. Messages made only of emoji,
  Unicode or from the server, show them larger.
- The panel and the quick reactions list all of a server's custom emoji. Before,
  only the first 200 loaded and the quick reactions offered 24. The next page is
  requested while the images of the previous one are stored, in low-priority
  background work, and pages are spaced to stay well within the server's rate
  limit; a page refused for the rate limit is asked again after a pause. The list
  is kept encrypted on the phone, so the panel shows it at once the next time;
  while it is read for the first time the panel shows animated placeholders
  instead of a list that keeps changing under your finger.

## Fixes

- The message box now reaches the bottom of the screen and sits right on top of
  the keyboard, without the empty band that used to appear below it.
- The names in **Com quem você quer falar?** are readable in the dark theme.
- The **Fechar** button of the notices shown over a conversation is easy to read.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications: the app only receives messages while it is open.
- The notification inbox, profile and status editing, user preferences, blocking
  users and server administration.
- Sound from a shared screen, per-person volume and Bluetooth headset selection
  in calls.
- Video attachments play only after being downloaded, and GIFs and animated
  emoji show only their first frame.

## Installing

Download the APK for your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow
installing apps from the app you opened it with.

- `arm64-v8a` is the right one for almost every phone sold in the last years.
- `armeabi-v7a` is for older 32-bit phones and `x86_64` for emulators and some
  tablets and Chromebooks.
- `universal` works on all of them and is larger.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of every file.
