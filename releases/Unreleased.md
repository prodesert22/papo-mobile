## Profile and account

- Edit your own profile from your profile or from the account settings:
  nickname, status message, description and typing phrase, plus your avatar and
  banner from the gallery. The editor is laid out like a profile card: the banner
  and the avatar show in place, each with a pencil button to change it, and the
  Save button sits next to the title, where it stays in view. A floating notice
  warns about unsaved changes and offers to discard them.
- Picking an avatar or banner opens an image editor: move and zoom the picture
  under a fixed frame (square for the avatar, 3:1 for the banner), rotate it, or
  reset it with "Tamanho". Pictures are shrunk on the phone and lose the location
  they carry, and the result of each change is shown. You can remove your banner.
  Animated pictures are not cropped.
- Choose Available, Away or Busy from the profile editor or from your own
  profile, with a disc in the color of each status.
- Block people from their profile and manage the list in the account settings.
  Blocking removes the direct conversation and stops direct messages in both
  directions; channel messages stay visible. The new conversation list hides the
  people you blocked and says so when the other person blocked you.
- Change your password from the account settings. Every session of the account
  ends, this one included, and the app takes you to sign in again.
- Reset your password with a link from the sign-in screen, with a warning when
  the link's address differs from the server's.
- The account settings show the server's details: name, icon, public or closed,
  owner, and how many members, channels and roles it has.

- The top of the channel list was reworked: the server name now has room and
  wraps to a second line instead of being cut off, the account settings button is
  now a vertical three-dot menu, and a new row under the name shows your avatar,
  a disc with the color of your status (green available, amber away, red busy,
  gray offline) and your name. Tapping that row opens the profile editor.

## Notifications

- A notification inbox for each account, opened from the bell at the top of the
  channel list: mentions, replies to your messages, `@everyone` and direct
  messages, with the reason for each one. Opening a notification takes you to the
  message and marks it as read, and you can mark all the loaded ones as read.
- Mention counts: a red number next to each channel and direct conversation, on
  each account in the server bar and on the bell. A channel with new messages
  shows its name in white and a dot on the right.
- Notification settings for each server, and for each channel and direct
  conversation (hold it in the list or use the menu in the conversation): which
  messages notify (all, mentions only or nothing), mute for 15 minutes up to 24
  hours or until you turn it back on, and hide `@everyone` or direct mentions.
  Channels use the server's choices until you change them. A muted channel is
  dimmed and loses its new-message dot, but its mention count stays. For now
  these settings stay on this phone.
- The conversation's search and pinned messages moved into the three-dot menu at
  the top, next to the new notification settings, so the conversation name has
  room again on narrow screens.
