## Notifications

- A notification inbox for each account, opened from the bell at the top of the
  channel list: mentions, replies to your messages, `@everyone` and direct
  messages, with the reason for each one. Opening a notification takes you to the
  message and marks it as read, and you can mark all the loaded ones as read.
- Mention counts: a red number next to each channel and direct conversation, on
  each account in the server bar and on the bell. A channel with new messages
  shows its name in white and a dot on the right.
- Notification settings for each server, and for each channel and direct
  conversation (hold it in the list or use the menu in the conversation): which
  messages notify (all, mentions only or nothing), mute for 15 minutes up to 24
  hours or until you turn it back on, and hide `@everyone` or direct mentions.
  Channels use the server's choices until you change them. A muted channel is
  dimmed and loses its new-message dot, but its mention count stays.
- A channel's notification level is saved on the server, so the terminal and
  web clients follow it, unless you turn off "Sincronizar com o servidor" for
  that channel or for the whole server: then the phone keeps its own level and
  the other clients keep theirs. Mute and hiding `@everyone` or mentions stay on
  this phone.
- System notifications while the app is open: mentions, replies, `@everyone`
  and direct messages show in the Android notification shade, one per
  conversation with the latest messages and each author's picture, grouped by
  account and following the notification settings. Tapping one opens the
  message, after the unlock if the app lock is on. Each account has its own
  Android notification channels (mentions and replies, direct messages,
  messages). The app asks for the notification permission once, on Android 13
  or later.
- Push notifications with the app closed or in the background, when the server
  has push turned on. Android shows them by itself: the level saved on the
  server decides which messages arrive, but mute and hiding `@everyone` or
  mentions do not apply to them. Tapping one opens the conversation, after the
  unlock if the app lock is on. Two accounts on the same server cannot both
  receive pushes on one phone.
- The conversation's search and pinned messages moved into the three-dot menu at
  the top, next to the new notification settings, so the conversation name has
  room again on narrow screens.
