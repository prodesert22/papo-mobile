## Messages

- While you type, a mention in the message field shows in the mention color on
  a light background, so you can see that the person will be notified;
  `@everyone` is highlighted too when you may use it.

## Fixes

- Mentioning someone whose username has a space, such as `@yog Sothoth`, now
  reaches them: the message is sent as a mention and they get the notification,
  instead of the name going out as plain text.
