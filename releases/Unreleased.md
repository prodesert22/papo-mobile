- Video attachments play inside the app while they download. Tap a video's card
  to watch it full screen, seek anywhere in it and turn the phone without losing
  your place; the video pauses when you leave the app. A video the phone cannot
  play, or one the server does not deliver, says why and offers **Baixar**.
- Videos from links play the same way: a link preview with a playable video
  shows a play button, and nothing is downloaded until you tap it. When the
  server cannot fetch the video the viewer says so and lets you try again; when
  the video is gone it offers to open the page in the browser.
