- The conversation is now a panel over the channel list, as in Discord. Swipe it
  to the right to see the channels, with a strip of the conversation left at the
  edge, and swipe back to the left, or tap the strip, to return. The panel
  follows your finger, can be caught in the middle of the movement, and settles
  open or closed when you let go. Opening a channel, the back button and the
  back gesture use the same panel.
- Each account remembers its own last conversation: switching accounts in the
  side rail switches the conversation waiting at the edge, and an account with
  none shows only its channel list.
- Messages that arrive in a conversation waiting at the edge stay unread in the
  list until you open it again.
- A conversation opened from a search result or from the call screen now opens
  in the panel, so going back shows the channel list instead of the search
  results or the call.
