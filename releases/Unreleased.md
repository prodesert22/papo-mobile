- Tap a person's avatar or name in a conversation, or the title of a direct
  conversation, to open their profile: banner, avatar, presence, status, roles,
  description and the date they joined. From there you can open a direct
  conversation with them or copy their `@username`.
- A member list for the server: the new button at the top of a channel opens a
  panel from the right edge with everyone grouped by presence and a search by
  name. Tap a person to open their profile. Swipe it back, tap outside it or
  press back to close it.
- Messages sent by a webhook now carry an **APP** badge next to the name and
  show the webhook's avatar, in conversations, pinned messages and search
  results. An author picture that is an address on another site is not loaded;
  those messages keep the initials.
- Fixed the avatar of a person who has none being requested from the server
  again every time it came into view.
