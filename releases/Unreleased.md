- In a channel where you may notify everyone, `@everyone` comes first when you
  type `@`. In messages, `@everyone` shows highlighted and the message turns
  gold only when its author may notify everyone, as in the web client.
- The app now follows the server's permissions. Channels you cannot read no
  longer show, and their messages are removed from the phone (a draft you had
  there is kept). A channel where you can only read shows that in place of the
  message box, and actions you may not take, such as attaching files, pinning,
  reacting, replying or joining a call, are no longer offered instead of failing
  with an error. Moderators and the owner can delete other people's messages
  from a message's actions, and your own reactions can always be taken back.
