- Record a voice message from the clip menu in a conversation: Papo asks for
  the microphone, records up to 30 minutes, and after you stop you can listen
  to it, discard it or send it. It is sent as its own audio message, and it
  plays here and on the web.
- The phone now keeps only the 120 newest messages of the 3 conversations you
  opened last on each account, with their images, GIFs and video posters.
  Older history and every other conversation are removed from the phone and
  fetched from the server when you open them. Videos were never stored and
  still are not. The first time you open the app after updating, the stored
  images of messages and other cached media are cleaned up; avatars and custom
  emojis stay.
